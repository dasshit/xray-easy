# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Xray Easy community a welcoming
and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, supportive,
and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected, and learning from
  mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or
  advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of
Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently ban a
contributor for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful.

Maintainers will consider reports fairly and confidentially, investigate them
promptly, and communicate the outcome to the people involved when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is representing
the project or its community in public. This includes using an official project
account, acting as a representative at an online or offline event, or otherwise
being identified as a project contributor. Project maintainers may clarify what
counts as representation of the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers using the
contact details available on their [GitHub profiles](https://github.com/dasshit).
Please include a description of what happened, when and where it happened, and
any relevant links or other context. Do not publish sensitive details in a
public issue or pull request. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy
when handling a report.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will choose an enforcement response based on the nature and impact
of the behavior. Possible responses include:

1. **Correction** — A private or public explanation of why the behavior was
   inappropriate, with a request to stop it.
2. **Warning** — A warning about the behavior and its consequences if it
   continues.
3. **Temporary ban** — A temporary ban from interacting with the project
   community after repeated or serious violations.
4. **Permanent ban** — A permanent ban from the project community for a pattern
   of violations, harassment, or conduct that seriously harms or threatens
   others.
